The firm

Coolidge Advisory is a data architecture and analytics consultancy built on more than a decade of enterprise-grade delivery across manufacturing, distribution, finance, legal, and operations functions, in the United States and internationally. A member of the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program, the firm specializes in Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and Azure data architecture. Our principal's career spans both the technical and operational dimensions of enterprise delivery, ranging from family-held and owner-operated businesses to large-scale enterprise operations. Coolidge Advisory brings that same rigor to every engagement regardless of scale.

We operate a deliberately principal-led model. Every engagement, from initial architecture through production deployment, is led and delivered by senior practitioners who build what they design. The person who sets the strategy is the same person writing the code and standing behind the results.

George Bommersheim
Founding Principal

George founded Coolidge Advisory on a conviction earned over more than a decade of enterprise delivery: the highest-quality data platforms are designed by people who also build them. Strategy without engineering produces slide decks. Engineering without strategy produces technical debt. This firm delivers both.

His career spans designing enterprise data platforms, leading full-lifecycle ERP and financial system migrations, and delivering analytics platforms for organizations across the United States, Europe, Australia, and New Zealand. Earlier in his career, he built and managed international supply chain and logistics operations across five European countries, including Switzerland, with direct experience in carrier integration, capacity planning, and cost optimization across global fulfillment networks.

George holds dual master’s degrees in Business Administration and Healthcare Administration, both earned with honors. He carries certifications in Business Intelligence (CSBI) and Lean Six Sigma (LSSGB). His background across both the technical and executive dimensions of data strategy is what allows Coolidge Advisory to engage at the C-suite level while delivering at the engineering level.
